# Code of Conduct

Be reasonable and keep it about the work.

- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Be patient with questions and respect people's time.
- Disagree without personal attacks, and respect moderation decisions.
- No harassment, slurs, threats, unwanted sexual attention, or sharing private information.
- Do not follow people across threads to keep an argument going.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other Hematite community spaces.

Report problems privately to a maintainer through a contact listed on their GitHub profile. Include a link and enough context to understand what happened. Maintainers will limit sharing to what is needed to handle the report.

Depending on the behaviour, maintainers may remove content, ask someone to stop, or restrict their participation. Reports involving a maintainer should go to another maintainer where possible.
